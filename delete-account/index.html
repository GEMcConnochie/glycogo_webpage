Delete your Glycogo account

Glycogo by GEM Performance lets you permanently delete your account and the personal data we store for you. This action cannot be undone.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address registered to your Glycogo account:

Email: contact@glycogo.fit
Subject line: Delete my Glycogo account
Include: the email address linked to your Glycogo account

We will verify ownership and complete deletion within 30 days. We may reply to confirm before processing.

Data deleted when your account is removed

When you delete your Glycogo account, we permanently remove the following from our active databases:

Data typeWhat happens
Account & sign-in Your Glycogo user account and authentication record are deleted.
Profile & settings Body weight, body fat %, macro targets, meal timing, sport settings, timezone, and Intervals.icu credentials stored in Glycogo.
Food & meal logs All logged meals, custom foods, meal templates, and draft/sync state.
Training data Training sessions and daily history/trends stored in Glycogo.
AI coach history Chat history stored on Glycogo servers (if any) is removed with your account.

Data that may be kept after deletion

Data typeRetention
Billing & subscriptions (Stripe) Payment and subscription records handled by Stripe may be retained by Stripe for legal, tax, and fraud-prevention purposes. Cancel your subscription before deleting if you no longer want to be billed. Glycogo does not delete Stripe customer records when you delete your app account.
Intervals.icu Workout or wellness data on Intervals.icu is not deleted by Glycogo. Remove or manage that data directly in your Intervals.icu account if needed.
Data on your device Cached app data on your phone or tablet (including saved sign-in tokens) remains until you uninstall the Glycogo app or clear the app’s storage in Android settings.
Infrastructure backups Our database provider may retain encrypted disaster-recovery backups for a limited period after deletion. These backups are not used for normal access and expire on the provider’s backup schedule.
Third-party AI processing Meal photos and chat messages sent for AI features are processed transiently by our AI providers during use. Glycogo does not store uploaded meal photos in your account database.
