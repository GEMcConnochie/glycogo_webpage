01 · TARGET

Set your intake target

Gut absorption of glucose alone tops out around 60 g/hour. Past that, pairing it with fructose, which uses a separate transporter, allows higher total intake. The ratio you need shifts as intake climbs, down to a floor of 1 : 0.8 at 120 g/h and above.

Below ~60 g/h, glucose alone typically covers demand — fructose is optional, not required.
