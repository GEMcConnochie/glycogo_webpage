Getting started with Glycogo

User guide for the Glycogo app at web.glycogo.fit

01

Log In

Create an account to save your data and enable syncing

Sign in at web.glycogo.fit with email and password or Google. Your food logs, foods, settings, and history sync to your account.

First launch runs a short onboarding for body composition, macros, and meals. Sports benchmarks and Intervals.icu are optional and can be added later in Settings.

Intervals.icu sync needs an account
Tap Save Day on Home to build Trends history
02

Navigate the App

Home, Trends, Foods, Settings, and floating action buttons

Tabs:

  • Home: day strip, macros, meals, training, within-day energy (today), Save Day, Carb Load
  • Trends: charts after Save Day
  • Foods: personal food library
  • Settings: profile, targets, sports, Intervals.icu, subscription

Floating buttons:

  • Add food: Search, Quick add, Barcode, Meal photo, Meal template
  • Add training: add a session for the selected day
  • AI Coach: nutrition chat (right side)

Log Food opens the meal builder: stage foods, pick meal and time, log to the diary.

03

Configure Settings

Core profile, meals, and optional sport refinements

Enter bodyweight and body fat % (or use the appearance estimator) to set fat-free mass and calorie targets.

Set Energy Availability, macro split, meal distribution, and training session slots.

Optionally under Sports & session targets, pick sports and add FTP, critical pace, swim CSS, efficiency, and carbs-per-hour bounds when you know them. The fueling heatmap previews in-session carbs by duration and RPE.

Optionally connect Intervals.icu and choose use device session calories.

See Settings Reference for parameter details.

04

Build Your Food Library

Personal foods on the Foods tab; search databases when logging

The Foods tab holds your personal library. Add custom foods with per-100g macros and serving sizes, or quick-log to a meal.

When logging, Search includes:

  • Your foods (always available)
  • Database (Open Food Facts)
  • Sports nutrition

Barcode and meal photo entry use the Add food FAB.

05

Add or Sync Training Sessions

Manual entry or automatic import via Intervals.icu

Add sessions with the Add training FAB, or sync via Sync Intervals / Sync training week on Home (Intervals.icu).

Manual entry: name, sport, time, duration, and session RPE (Borg CR100, 1–100). The slider shows a short label. Rate the whole session, not peak intervals only.

RPE sets carbs and calories per hour during the workout; duration scales during-session totals.

Synced RPE uses, in order:

  • Completed: power vs FTP, Intervals RPE, heart rate, other intensity fields
  • Planned: planned power vs FTP, planned pace/speed vs critical pace or CSS, Intervals RPE

Edit synced RPE if needed. Use High-carb fuelling for upper-range during-session carbs, or Merge sessions when multiple sessions share a day.

See RPE Reference and the FAQ.
06

Plan and Log Nutrition

Templates, smart suggestions, and flexible entry

Open Log Food from the FAB, a meal card, or Foods. Stage items, set meal and time, then Log to diary.

  • Suggest quantities fills remaining macros for the current meal
  • Meal templates reload saved combinations
  • Check a meal card to mark complete (locks intake and redistributes targets to open meals)
  • Carb Load on Home raises baseline carbs for that weekday; training fuel still adds on top
  • Within-day energy chart on today shows cumulative balance from meals and training

All Foods bulk-edits the log; Reset day clears the selected date.

AI

AI Nutrition Assistant

Context-aware coaching, photo logging, and meal suggestions

New

Tap the AI Coach FAB to chat with context from your sessions, targets, and log.

Fast-log food

Describe a meal in text and add it to your log.

Photo estimate

Identify foods and estimate macros from a photo.

Meal suggestions

Ideas based on remaining targets; add from chat.

Fuelling advice

What and when to eat around training.

08

Save Your Day & Track Trends

Build a history and watch your fuelling patterns over time

Tap Save Day on Home to store the day in history. View charts on the Trends tab (30 / 90 days or all time). Optionally push totals to Intervals.icu wellness fields.

Reset day clears the food log for the selected date.
