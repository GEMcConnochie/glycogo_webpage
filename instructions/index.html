Getting started with Glycogo

60s

1-minute quick start

  1. Sign in on the app or at web.glycogo.fit and finish onboarding.
  2. Add today's training (or sync from your device).
  3. Log meals with Log Food.
  4. Mark each meal complete once you've eaten it.
  5. Tap Save Day when you're done.
Three action buttons sit at the bottom of every screen. Add training Add food / Log Food AI Coach
01

Log In

Save your data and sync across devices

Your logs, foods, settings, and history stay linked to your account so you can pick up on any device.

  • Sign in on the app or at web.glycogo.fit (email or Google).
  • First launch runs short onboarding for body composition, targets, and meals.
  • Syncing workouts from your device requires a free Intervals.icu account.
02

Configure Settings

Personalise your calorie and carbohydrate targets

Glycogo personalises calorie and carbohydrate recommendations using your body composition, training, and performance data. A few minutes here makes your daily targets much more accurate.

Basic settings

  • Body weight and body fat % (or use Estimate BF%)
  • Energy Availability
  • Macro split (%)
  • Meal distribution and default meal times
  • Training session slots and pre-session snacks

Sport settings

  • Select the sports and activities you train (running, cycling, swimming, gym, team sports, winter sports, and more)
  • Add custom sports with your own calorie ranges if needed
  • Carbohydrates per hour (lower / upper)
  • Minimum duration for carbohydrates: during-session fuel only applies above this unless High-carb fuelling is on for that session.
  • Recommended: Enter critical power/paces, and estimated efficiency for running, cycling, and swimming to dial in your RPE estimations and refine calorie estimates when Use device session calories is off.

Device sync

  • Connect your device via an Intervals.icu account to sync workouts
  • Turn on Use device session calories to use synced device burn data

See the Settings Reference for Energy Availability zones and other parameters.

03

Build Your Food Library

Save foods you eat often

A personal library means less searching every time you log.

  • Add custom foods on the Foods tab, via Quick add, or a barcode scan.
  • Search an extensive online database and sports nutrition products and add them to your favourites.
04

Add or Sync Training Sessions

Training drives your fuel targets

Glycogo adjusts daily calories and carbohydrates based on what you train. Without sessions, targets stay at your rest-day baseline.

  • Add manually with Add training , or your training day or week from your device on Home.
  • Session RPE (1–100): rate the whole workout, not peak intervals only.
  • RPE sets carbohydrates and calories per hour; duration scales the during-session total.
  • High-carb fuelling and Merge sessions are available when editing a session.

See the RPE Reference below.

05

Plan and Log Nutrition

Hit your targets meal by meal

Logging shows how much you still need to eat today and updates as training changes your targets and you complete meals.

  • Open Log Food from a meal card, the Foods tab, or Add food .
  • Suggest quantities fills remaining macros for the meal you are building.
  • Meal templates reload saved food combinations and serving sizes.
  • Carb Load on Home raises baseline carbohydrates for that weekday; session fuel still adds on top.
Mark a meal complete once you've eaten it. Glycogo locks that meal and automatically redistributes your remaining calories and macros across the rest of the day.
AI

AI Nutrition Assistant

Fast logging and fuelling advice

The coach sees your sessions, targets, and current log, so suggestions fit your day rather than generic advice.

Open AI Coach to fast-log food, get meal ideas and recipes, or ask about fuelling around training.

07

Save Your Day & Track Trends

Build history over time

Save Day stores your completed nutrition and training data in Trends. You can still go back and edit yesterday's log if you need to.

  • Charts on the Trends tab appear after you save a few days.
  • Reset day clears the food log for the selected date.
REF

Nutrition Settings Reference

Parameter descriptions

Energy and Macronutrient Targets

ParameterDescription
Energy Availabilitykcal/kg FFM Calories available for body functions after accounting for exercise cost:
  • 25–30: Low. Prolonged use is associated with REDs and impaired recovery.
  • 30–40: Moderate. Appropriate for weight loss phases.
  • 40–50: Optimal for most athletes.
  • 50–55: High. Suited to heavy training blocks and growth phases.
Macro split (%)Carbohydrate, protein, and fat as a share of daily energy. Must total 100%.
Carb LoadWeekday toggle on Home for higher baseline carbohydrates before an event. Session fuel still adds on top.

Session and Sport-Specific Parameters

ParameterDescription
Selected sports & activitiesRunning, cycling, swimming, MET-based activities (fitness, sports, water, winter, outdoor), and custom sports you define.
Custom sport kcal/hrEasy and max calories per hour for a sport you add yourself.
Critical power / paceThreshold power (cycling), critical speed (running), or swimming critical speed. Sets the high end of sport calorie estimates. Can be entered directly or calculated from time trials.
Easy power / paceEasy-ride power (cycling), easy run pace, or easy swim pace. Sets the low end of sport calorie estimates.
EfficiencyGross efficiency (cycling), running efficiency, and propulsive efficiency (swimming). Adjusts calorie burn at a given power or pace.
Carbohydrates per hour (lower / upper)Lower and upper bounds for during-session carbohydrates by effort.
Minimum duration for carbohydratesDuring-session carbohydrates apply only when a session is longer than this. High-carb fuelling on a session overrides this.
Use device session caloriesWhen enabled, synced sessions from your device use calorie data with resting expenditure subtracted.

RPE Reference Scale

Session effort ratings based on the Borg CR100 Scale®

Session RPE reflects overall session difficulty on the Borg CR100 scale®. It sets target carbohydrates and calories per hour during exercise.

RPE Label Typical feel
0–10Very EasyRecovery effort
10–20EasyComfortable conversation
20–30Steady EasyLight endurance
30–40ModerateComfortable aerobic
40–50Moderately HardNoticeable effort
50–60StrongPurposeful training
60–70HardDemanding but sustainable
70–80Very HardThreshold-style efforts or hard intervals
80–90Extremely HardV02 interals with limited recovery or an intense prolonged effort
90–100Maximal Session LoadRace or race-like effort
If recoveries, warm-up, and cool-down are logged as part of the session, overall RPE will be lower vs peak rep RPE. More in the FAQ.
