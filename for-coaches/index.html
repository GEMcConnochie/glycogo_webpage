For coaches and sports nutrition practitioners

Know how your athletes are fuelling.

See what athletes are eating, how their intake stacks up against their requirements and whether they're getting enough carbohydrate to support their training. Glycogo brings nutrition, training and wellness data into one place, so you can assess the detail behind an athlete's daily fuelling.

01. Athlete roster

Nutrition across your squad, at a glance.

Compare daily energy and macronutrient intake with each athlete's targets. See how nutrition changes across the week alongside planned training and wellness ratings, and identify athletes whose intake may need a closer look.

02. Individual nutrition settings

Set targets that reflect the athlete.

Every athlete has different requirements. Set individual energy availability targets, macronutrient requirements, meal distributions and snack targets.

Carbohydrate requirements are based on the demands of each session, including duration and intensity, with targets for daily intake, pre-training and fuelling during exercise. Apply settings individually or across athletes with similar requirements.

1 / 4

03. Daily nutrition and session fuelling

See what's being eaten, and when.

Daily totals don't tell you the whole story. An athlete might meet their energy target but still be underfuelling around key sessions, or leave too much of their intake until the end of the day.

Review individual meals against nutrition targets, including energy availability throughout the day. Planned training sessions add context to when athletes are eating and how their intake lines up with the demands of exercise.

1 / 2

04. Trends and monitoring

Put nutrition data alongside training and wellness.

See how energy availability, carbohydrate and macronutrient intake change over time, relative to each athlete's targets.

Compare these trends with training load and wellness measures, including sleep, fatigue, hunger and soreness. Nutrition data is easier to interpret when you can see what else is happening in an athlete's training week.

1 / 3

05. Race planning

Take the guesswork out of race fuelling.

Build a race plan around the course, expected finish time and intensity. Set carbohydrate, fluid, sodium and caffeine targets, then allocate products and servings across the race.

Account for aid stations, sweat rate and weather conditions. Import a GPX course or an Intervals.icu workout to build the plan around the demands of the event.

The finished plan includes a timed fuelling schedule, total nutrition requirements and the amount of each product needed.

1 / 4

06. Training integration and session planning

Match fuelling requirements to the training.

Import planned and completed sessions from Intervals.icu, or add sessions directly in Glycogo.

For each session, Glycogo estimates carbohydrate and fat utilisation using the athlete's individual characteristics, exercise intensity and duration, diet and carbohydrate consumed during exercise. These estimates feed into daily energy and carbohydrate requirements, so targets reflect the training being done.

07. Messages and athlete management

Keep the conversation close to the data.

Messages

Discuss nutrition, training and wellness directly with athletes. Messages can be linked to individual days, keeping the conversation alongside the relevant information.

Athlete access

Create athlete profiles and set their nutrition requirements before they register. Athletes join using an invitation code, and access can be revoked when they leave your group.
