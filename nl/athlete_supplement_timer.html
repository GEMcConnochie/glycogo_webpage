Wetenschappelijke bronnen en evidence-notities

De timingstrategieën komen uit peer-reviewed literatuur, gezamenlijke standpunten en consensusrichtlijnen.

  • Overkoepelende consensus: Maughan, R. J., et al. (2018). IOC Consensus Statement: Dietary supplements and the high-performance athlete. British Journal of Sports Medicine, 52(7), 439-455. DOI: 10.1136/bjsports-2017-099027.
  • Wei-eiwit en caseïne: Morton, R. W., et al. (2018). A systematic review, meta-analysis and meta-regression of the effect of protein supplementation on resistance training-induced gains. BJSM. DOI: 10.1136/bjsports-2017-097608; Kerksick, C. M., et al. (2018). ISSN position stand: nutrient timing. JISSN. DOI: 10.1186/s12970-017-0189-4.
  • Caseïne voor het slapen: Res, P. T., et al. (2012). Protein ingestion before sleep improves postexercise overnight recovery. Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise. DOI: 10.1249/MSS.0b013e31824cc363; Trommelen, J., & van Loon, L. J. (2016). Pre-sleep protein ingestion to improve skeletal muscle adaptive response. Nutrients. DOI: 10.3390/nu8120763.
  • Collageen en vitamine C: Shaw, G., et al. (2017). Vitamin C-enriched gelatin supplementation before intermittent activity augments collagen synthesis. American Journal of Clinical Nutrition. DOI: 10.3945/ajcn.116.138594.
  • Vitamine C en afgezwakte adaptaties: Ristow, M., et al. (2009). Antioxidants prevent health-promoting effects of physical exercise in humans. PNAS. DOI: 10.1073/pnas.0903485106; Merry, T. L., & Ristow, M. (2016). Do antioxidant supplements interfere with adaptations? Journal of Physiology. DOI: 10.1113/JP270654.
  • Cafeïne: Spriet, L. L. (2014). Exercise and sport performance with low doses of caffeine. Sports Medicine. DOI: 10.1007/s40279-014-0257-8.
  • Creatine: Antonio, J., & Ciccone, V. (2013). The effects of pre versus post workout supplementation of creatine monohydrate. JISSN. DOI: 10.1186/1550-2783-10-36; Kreider, R. B., et al. (2017). ISSN position stand: creatine. JISSN. DOI: 10.1186/s12970-017-0173-z.
  • Bèta-alanine: Trexler, E. T., et al. (2015). ISSN position stand: Beta-Alanine. JISSN. DOI: 10.1186/s12970-015-0090-y.
  • Voedingsnitraat / rode biet: Jones, A. M. (2014). Dietary nitrate supplementation and exercise performance. Sports Medicine. DOI: 10.1007/s40279-014-0149-y.
  • Natriumbicarbonaat: Carr, A. J., et al. (2011). Effect of sodium bicarbonate on [HCO3-], pH, and gastrointestinal symptoms. IJSNEM. DOI: 10.1123/ijsnem.21.3.189.
  • IJzerregulatie: Sim, M., et al. (2019). Iron considerations for the athlete: a narrative review. European Journal of Applied Physiology. DOI: 10.1007/s00421-019-04157-y.
  • Calcium en botgezondheid: Sale, C., & Elliott-Sale, K. J. (2019). Nutrition and Athlete Bone Health. Sports Medicine. DOI: 10.1007/s40279-019-01161-2.
  • Vitamine D3: Close, G. L., et al. (2013). The effects of vitamin D3 supplementation on serum total 25[OH]D concentration and physical performance: a randomised dose-response study. British Journal of Sports Medicine. DOI: 10.1136/bjsports-2012-091735.
  • Omega-3: Philpott, J. D., et al. (2019). Applications of omega-3 polyunsaturated fatty acid supplementation for sport performance. Research in Sports Medicine. DOI: 10.1080/15438627.2018.1550401.
  • Magnesium: Volpe, S. L. (2015). Magnesium and the Athlete. Current Sports Medicine Reports. DOI: 10.1249/JSR.0000000000000178.
  • Zink: Prasad, A. S. (2013). Discovery of Human Zinc Deficiency: Its Impact on Human Health and Disease. Advances in Nutrition. DOI: 10.3945/an.112.003210.

Antidoping en veiligheid: Supplementen kennen contaminatierisico's die tot positieve dopingtests kunnen leiden. Topsporters zouden alleen partijgeteste producten (bijv. Informed-Sport of HASTA) moeten gebruiken en ingrediënten tegen verboden lijsten moeten controleren. Deze planner optimaliseert alleen de timing en schrijft geen doseringen voor.
